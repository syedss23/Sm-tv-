Mehmed: Fetihler Sultanı Season 1 Episode 12 Review – When Everything Goes Wrong At Once

September 27, 2026 by SmTv Urdu
Mehmed: Fetihler Sultanı
Season 1 · Episode 12
Series: Mehmed: Fetihler Sultanı
Season 1, Episode 12
Original Air Date: May 21, 2024 (TRT1, Turkey)
Lead Cast: Serkan Çayoğlu as Sultan Mehmed, Tuba Ünsal as Mara Hatun
Genre: Historical Drama, War, Political Intrigue

Introduction

While the previous episode's finale saw Mehmed's cannon successfully passing its tests, the following one begins with the same weapon blowing up due to the technical difficulties and taking several workers with it. Episode 12 of Mehmed: Fetihler Sultanı is one of the most downbeat ones of the season, as the young sultan has to deal with several setbacks at once, and it all serves to make his character even stronger.

Season 1 Episode 12 Recap: Disaster, Abduction, and an Ultimatum

The cannon disaster occurs in the very beginning of the episode, and it already serves as a major setback for Mehmed, who was just getting ready to lead the siege. The cannon, which is meant to be his technological and military superiority over Constantinople, has failed him at the most undeserved time, and the young sultan is furious about it. The cannon's explosion, which occurs right after the cannon successfully demonstrated its power at the very beginning of episode 11, is yet another sign that Mehmed's life is full of disappointments and failures that he has to overcome on his way to becoming a great leader.

Outside of the cannon troubles, Mehmed now has to deal with his wife being kidnapped by Konstantinos, who is trying to use her as leverage in the ongoing negotiations with the Ottoman Empire. Even though she is kidnapped and held for ransom, Mara Hatun proves to be as strong as ever, refusing to yield to Konstantinos's advances and attempts to convince her to side with him. However, the kidnapping serves as yet another setback for Mehmed, who has to deal with internal and external issues at the same time. Internal issues seem to be endless as well, as İshak Paşa is still suspicious of Çandarlı Halil Paşa, and he believes that the latter is manipulating him and the sultan in general.

Elsewhere, Bali Bey is recovering from the assassination attempt, and his friends are trying to track down those responsible for it. It becomes obvious that the enemies of Mehmed are getting stronger and more numerous with every passing day, and they are targeting not only the sultan but also some of his closest allies and advisors.

However, Mehmed does not break under the pressure of all those troubles. In fact, he seems to be growing stronger with every passing day and issuing more orders to his enemies. First of all, Mehmed sends Evrenosoğlu Ali to the capital to deliver a message to Konstantinos about Mara Hatun and the ongoing siege preparations. Then, back in the capital, Bahar Hatun is trying to fill the power vacuum left by Mara Hatun, who is currently in captivity. It is also made clear that Hala Sultan is constantly watching Bahar Hatun, making sure that she does not do anything suspicious. While this episode does not make the harem the center of the plot, it still demonstrates the power dynamics between the sultan's wives.

Season 1 Episode 12 Characters

This episode serves to demonstrate that Mehmed is growing stronger with every passing day and that nothing, not even multiple setbacks, can break him. The young sultan is becoming more powerful and is taking more responsibilities, including those related to military ones. His reaction to the cannon disaster, the kidnapping of his wife, and the internal power struggles within the empire demonstrates that he is becoming an impressive leader. His orders to Evrenosoğlu Ali show that Mehmed is not afraid of challenges and that he will deal with them on his own terms.

Mara Hatun is also a strong character, and her kidnapping and subsequent behavior while in the hands of her captors prove that she is as strong as she has always been. Even though she is being used as leverage, she refuses to yield to pressure and continue being a tool for Konstantinos's political ambitions.

Season 1 Episode 12 Historical Background

The early cannons were rather unstable, and it was not uncommon for them to explode due to technical failures. It was a rather frequent occurrence for the weapons designed and built by Urban to explode at the moment of firing or right after, and the cannon disaster depicted in this episode is entirely plausible. The cannon that was built to be the largest in the world at the time was bound to have technical issues, and those issues could have been fatal.

Season 1 Episode 12 Our Review

This episode is one of the darkest ones in the season, as Mehmed has to deal with a series of setbacks and failures at once. First of all, he loses his wife, who is kidnapped by his enemies. Then, he loses his cannon, which is a powerful symbol of his military might and his ability to protect his empire. Finally, Mehmed has to deal with the growing internal dissent within his empire, as several of his advisors suspect each other of being traitors and spies. However, Mehmed's reaction to all of those troubles demonstrates that he is a strong leader who does not break under pressure. The young sultan grows even stronger with every challenge, and his orders to Evrenosoğlu Ali show that he is willing to take direct and uncompromising action against his enemies.

⭐ Rating: 8.5/10 – A dark episode in which Mehmed has to deal with a series of setbacks, but those setbacks only make him stronger.
