Common questions
FAQ
How do I pay?

If you are in India, UAE, Saudi Arabia or the UK, pay with UPI (scan the QR or copy the UPI ID) and enter the plan amount manually. In all other countries, choose "Other countries" and pay with PayPal or a debit/credit card.

What happens after payment?

Tap "Activate on Telegram" and send your payment screenshot. Your access will be activated after quick verification — usually instant. You will get all episodes on Telegram.

Why is there an 8% charge on international payments?

PayPal charges a fee on international payments, and the money is converted from USD to INR. The 8% covers these costs so the plan value matches the Indian price. UPI payments have no extra charge.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. In "Other countries", choose the Debit or Credit Card button. You do not need a PayPal account.

Payment shows "risk" or fails?

For UPI, try a different UPI app or scan the QR from inside your app. For card or PayPal, try another card or retry after a few minutes. Contact us on Telegram if the issue persists.

Refunds and cancellations

Subscriptions are non-refundable. You can stop renewal at any time — your current period stays active until it expires.

Support hours

Daily 10:00–22:00 IST. Messages sent after hours are answered first thing the next day.
